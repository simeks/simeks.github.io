## Trees

One thing I've done though, I finished up the trees for the most part. They are procedurally generated through a little Lua setup I created. The script generates stamps, which is basically a pre-computed voxel volume, that you then stamp into the world. So the trees are pre-generated and I only pay the cost of copying them into the voxel world when I place them.

The Lua script part is especially nice. The Lua script is basically just a recipe for what makes a tree, like place sphere there, cone there, etc. It evaluates fairly quickly but the nice part is that I can easily adjust it and just reload the whole thing while the game is running.
